Fix TrainChat subscription entitlement failure.

Context:
Lisa Jones bought the $49.99/month TrainChat subscription. Stripe shows the subscription/payment, but TrainChat still treats her account as free.

Audit result:
The root failure appears to be Stripe entitlement sync, not frontend gating. On server boot, StripeSync fails during managed webhook initialization:

error: relation "stripe.accounts" does not exist
at StripeSync.findOrCreateManagedWebhook
at initStripe (artifacts/api-server/src/index.ts)

Because this error is swallowed as a warning, the app boots but webhooks never reliably register/process, so paid Stripe subscriptions do not update the users table.
