```text

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
TrainChat records the outcome
      ↓
A sanitized outcome event is sent asynchronously to Kevin
      ↓
Kevin learns without becoming the source of truth for the workout
```

Kevin must enrich TrainChat, not control it.
